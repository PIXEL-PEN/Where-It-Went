Where It Went is a simple, opinionated expense tracker that does one thing well — tells you where your money went.

Unlike most financial apps, it deliberately excludes income tracking. Your bank handles that. This app focuses entirely on spending.

Two separate modules keep your numbers honest:

Daily Living tracks recurring household expenses — groceries, rent, utilities, transport. Categories are tagged as Fixed, Necessity, or Discretionary, giving you a clear picture of your spending profile each month.

Accounts tracks one-off budget envelopes — home renovations, travel, projects. These are kept completely separate so they never skew your monthly Daily Living totals.

Features:
- Quick expense entry with category tags
- View spending by Month, Date, or Category
- Distribution pie chart across Fixed, Necessity and Discretionary
- Monthly account totals on the Distribution page
- Export to CSV or HTML
- Fully offline — no ads, no tracking, no cloud

Simple by design. Honest by nature.
