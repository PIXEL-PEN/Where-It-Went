
## Where It Went — Quick Guide

Where It Went helps you understand your spending without budgets, income tracking, or complexity.

You log what you spend. The app organizes it. That’s it.

---

### Daily Living

This is your everyday spending.

Entries are grouped by month and category. Each category has a tag:

* Fixed
* Necessity
* Discretionary
* Off-Budget

These tags power the Distribution graph, helping you see how your spending changes over time.

Off-Budget items are excluded from the graph — ideal for trips or special situations that shouldn’t distort normal life.

---

### Accounts

Accounts are for spending that doesn’t belong in normal monthly life.

You can create:

* Projects
* Travel
* Custom accounts

Each account stands on its own and does not affect Daily Living totals or the Distribution graph.

Accounts can be expanded or collapsed.
If a filter is active, a small ✕ indicator appears in the top bar — tap it to return to the full view.

Archived accounts remain visible in summaries but no longer accept new entries.

Deleting an account removes it together with all of its entries.

---

### Yearly Summary

Open it from the menu, or from Summaries → Yearly summaries.

Each year has a banner with its total. Your year starts in the month of your first entry, so if you began in January you'll see January to December; if you began in May, May to April.

While a year is still in progress, the banner shows the months so far and when the year ends.

Tap a banner to open it. Daily Living and Accounts can each be opened to show their category totals, and each account can be opened to show its own categories.

Use Export › to save a year's totals as a CSV file or send them by email.

---

### Backup and Restore

Settings → Export → Full backup saves everything in one file: Daily Living, Accounts, your categories and their tags, and your currency. Keep a recent copy somewhere safe.

Settings → Import restores a full backup. It can also bring in an "All items" CSV export from Daily Living or Accounts.

Before importing, you'll see what the file contains. Entries already in the app are skipped and nothing is deleted, so importing the same file twice is harmless. A full backup also restores your category tags; CSV exports don't include tags, so categories they add start as Fixed.

Settings → Reset lets you delete Daily Living entries, Accounts, or both. Make a full backup first.

---

### Your Data

Everything stays on your device.

No ads.
No cloud.
No tracking.

Keep entries simple and consistent, and Where It Went will give you a clear picture of where your money went.

Feedback is always welcome: you'll find the email address in Settings → About.
